The Coin Knight
A pixel art adventure
By Sullydux

Embark on a shiny quest

The Coin Knight is a pixel art platformer game where you, the knight, collects coins and fights slimes. At the end fight the boss!

About the Game

This is a 2D pixel art game I made for fun. It is my first Godot game, so I was and am still learning. It is available as a Windows, Linux, or Mac app and on a website. It is using Godot 4.0.

Updates

This is still in development so more features are going to be added soon! Email me if you have any suggestions or found any bugs. I am open to constructive criticism.

Be a Hero But Also Beware

Explore a handcrafted pixel art world packed with challenges and hidden secrets, all within a tightly designed map. Coins are scattered throughout the environment, rewarding careful exploration and risk-taking. Scattered flags act as checkpoints, letting you push forward without losing progress when you fall. The realm is under siege by slimes and their fearsome boss, causing all citizens to flee. You will face the challenges alone.
